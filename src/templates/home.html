Who we are

We are Human Right and Disability Right Focus Organisation

Supertouch Kindness Foundation (SKF), is a Human right and disability rights non – governmental organization. A beacon of hope in Nigeria and beyond. We are not just an organization; we are a movement dedicated to empowering and uplifting the Underprivileged. At SKF, we recognize that true transformation is driven by acts of kindness and boundless empathy. Our aim is to create a profound and durable impact on the lives of the less privileged.

What we do

Thematic Areas

Our work is built around core thematic areas designed to meet urgent needs and foster long-term impact. From health and protection to empowerment and advocacy, each area reflects our commitment to inclusive, community-led development.

Disability Inclusion

We advocate for the rights and inclusion of people with disabilities, ensuring their voices are heard and needs are addressed in all community initiatives.

Protection

Our protection programs focus on supporting survivors of gender-based violence (GBV), mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), and mine action awareness

Health

We provide basic healthcare services referrals, hygiene education, and SRH awareness — especially in hard-to-reach communities with limited access to medical support.

Education

We promote access to quality education for children, youth, and women, and empower communities with knowledge through awareness campaigns and training.

Climate Change

We engage in environmental cleanup, water access projects, and community sensitization on disaster preparedness and environmental sustainability.

Good Governance

We strengthen grassroots participation, promote transparency, and work with local institutions to ensure responsive governance that meets community needs.

How we work

How We Work With Communities

We take a grassroots approach, working directly with local leaders, volunteers, and organizations to co-create sustainable solutions. Our model focuses on community engagement, capacity building, and inclusive development to ensure lasting impact.

Learn more
Community Engagement & Needs Assessment

We start by listening — working closely with community members to identify their most pressing needs and priorities.

Program Design & Local Partnership

Our team co-creates tailored programs with local stakeholders, ensuring community ownership and cultural sensitivity.

Implementation & Capacity Building

We deliver services and training on the ground, empowering individuals with the skills and tools to improve their own lives.

Monitoring, Evaluation & Sustainability

We track progress, adapt where needed, and ensure that impact continues long after the project ends.
